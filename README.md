## DESIGN DIRECTION: PREMIUM FIRST IMPRESSION
This site should feel notably more upscale and polished than a typical local plumber site — the goal is for the business owner to be genuinely impressed on first look, not just informed. Specifically:
- Lean into a **luxurious, premium visual style**: rich dark backgrounds with metallic gold or brushed-steel accents, elegant serif or high-end sans-serif headings, generous white space, and high-quality, polished imagery rather than plain stock photos.
- Avoid anything that reads as a generic template — the layout, spacing, and hero section should feel custom and intentional.
- Include **subtle motion/animation** throughout, so the site feels alive rather than static:
  - Smooth fade-ins and slide-ups as sections scroll into view
  - A subtle animated hero (e.g. slowly panning background image, gently pulsing CTA button, or a soft particle/water-ripple effect appropriate to a plumbing brand)
  - Hover animations on service cards and buttons (slight lift/scale/glow on hover)
  - Animated counters for stats (e.g. "4.7★ Rating," "24 Hour Availability," "X+ Happy Customers" counting up on scroll)
  - Smooth-scroll navigation between sections
- Motion should feel premium and understated — smooth easing, not flashy or distracting. The overall impression should be "this plumber invested in quality," not "this is a stock plumber website."
